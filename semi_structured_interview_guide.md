# Semi-Structured Expert Interview Guide

**Version:** v1.0.0

## Opening

Thank you for reviewing this framework. The purpose is to evaluate module weights, classification boundaries, and safeguards. The interview does not ask you to determine whether any individual committed misconduct.

## Questions

1. Which evidence modules should carry the greatest weight for biomedical experimental papers?
2. Which modules should carry greater weight for clinical or epidemiological papers?
3. Should author-history evidence be weighted differently from article-level evidence?
4. Under what conditions should C5 be allowed?
5. Under what conditions should C6 be allowed?
6. Should C7 ever override a high TRS case?
7. How should official retraction wording be interpreted?
8. What safeguards are needed to avoid same-name author errors?
9. What model outputs would be ethically unsafe?
10. What would make the framework more useful for reviewers, editors, or research-integrity offices?

## Closing

Ask the expert to complete the weight survey template and optionally comment on synthetic benchmark cases.
