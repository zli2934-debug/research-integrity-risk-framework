# Ethical Guidelines

1. Do not use this framework to publicly accuse individuals.
2. Do not equate retraction with fraud.
3. Do not equate public comments with official findings.
4. Preserve uncertainty and alternative explanations.
5. Confirm author identity before author-history analysis.
6. Distinguish paper-level risk from person-level responsibility.
7. Use synthetic cases for demonstrations whenever possible.
8. Do not classify intent without official wording or strong multi-source evidence.
9. Treat the framework as decision support, not adjudication.
10. Avoid using the framework for harassment, reputational attacks, or online trials by public opinion.

Recommended disclaimer:

> This framework does not determine guilt, intent, or personal misconduct. It organizes public records and data-integrity signals for further review.
