# Candidate Title and Abstract

## Recommended title

**A Formal-Logic and Weighted-Evidence Framework for Assessing Research Integrity Risk in Scholarly Articles**

## Alternative titles

1. **Research Integrity Risk Framework: An Open-Source Toolkit for Modular Evidence Scoring and Retraction-Aware Literature Assessment**
2. **From Retraction Records to Risk Signals: A Modular Framework for Evidence-Based Research Integrity Assessment**
3. **A Transparent Rule-Based Framework for Research Integrity Risk Assessment in Biomedical Literature**

## Draft abstract

Research integrity concerns are increasingly visible in the scholarly literature, yet existing public records and post-publication discussions are often difficult to translate into a transparent, reproducible, and ethically bounded assessment workflow. This manuscript introduces the Research Integrity Risk Framework, an open-source, alpha-stage methodology for organizing research-integrity evidence using modular scoring, formal-logic safeguards, database evidence mapping, and synthetic benchmark tests. The framework decomposes scholarly articles into nine evidence modules, including figures, tables, statistics, raw data, methods, claims, external database records, author-history records, and responsibility attribution. Each module is scored on a 0–5 scale and combined into a Total Risk Score using pilot expert-prior weights. Hard logic overrides separate preliminary risk assessment from formal correction, retraction, confirmed misconduct, repeated misconduct pattern, and possible non-intentional error or inconclusive retraction categories. The framework also includes Retraction Watch CSV integration for evidence organization, while explicitly distinguishing name matching from author-identity confirmation and database records from misconduct adjudication. Synthetic benchmark cases are used to test rule implementation, but the current version is not empirically calibrated and should not be used as a final decision system. The framework is intended as a transparent decision-support and documentation tool for preliminary research-integrity risk assessment, not as a replacement for journal, institutional, or legal review.
