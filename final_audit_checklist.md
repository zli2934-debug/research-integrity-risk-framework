# Final Audit Checklist

**Current package version:** v1.0.0

This checklist supports a third-party-style final review before v1.0 or manuscript submission.

## Source accuracy

- Are all factual claims linked to verifiable sources?
- Are Retraction Watch, Crossref, Retraction Database, journal notices, PubPeer, and institutional notices clearly distinguished?
- Are database records separated from official misconduct findings?

## Formal logic

- Does the report avoid `retraction = fraud` reasoning?
- Does the report avoid `PubPeer comment = proof` reasoning?
- Does the report avoid treating author-name matches as identity confirmation?
- Does C6 require multiple formal records, same-author confirmation, repeated pattern, clear responsibility, and official misconduct-type wording?

## Quantitative checks

- Do all weights sum to 1.0?
- Are TRS calculations reproducible?
- Are C7 safeguards active for high TRS or high core-module abnormalities?
- Has sensitivity analysis been run for borderline cases?

## Ethics and misuse risk

- Does the output avoid public accusation language?
- Does it preserve uncertainty and alternative explanations?
- Does it distinguish paper-level risk from person-level responsibility?
- Does it state that the framework is not an official investigation substitute?

## Final outcome

Decision: `Verified / Needs Revision / Unsupported / Risky Claim`
