# Synthetic Case 02: Hard Logic Override

This is a synthetic example for demonstration only.

## Scenario

A paper has a moderate TRS of 2.10. However, the journal has issued a formal retraction notice explicitly stating that the article was retracted due to image manipulation.

## Interpretation

Even though the TRS is only moderate, the official wording triggers C5.

## Classification

C5 Confirmed misconduct.

## Reason

Official notice explicitly states misconduct-type wording: image manipulation.
