# Limitations

- Database coverage is incomplete and may lag behind publisher records.
- Retraction notices may be vague or legally constrained.
- Retraction does not automatically imply intentional misconduct.
- Same-name authors can cause false author-history matches.
- Module scores require human judgment and may vary across raters.
- Current weights are pilot expert-prior weights, not empirically validated optimal weights.
- Image analysis depends on image quality, availability of raw images, and specialized expertise.
- TRS cannot infer intent by itself.
- C5 and C6 classifications require hard logic conditions beyond weighted scoring.
- The framework should be calibrated against benchmark cases before high-stakes use.


## v0.4 Database Limitations

Retraction Watch CSV integration improves evidence organization but does not solve identity disambiguation, incomplete notices, vague retraction reasons, or responsibility attribution. The Retraction Database web interface may require manual checking and is not assumed to be programmatically stable.
