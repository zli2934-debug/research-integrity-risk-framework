# Interview Ethics and Consent Notes

**Component introduced:** v1.0.0  
**Current package version:** v1.0.0

## Purpose

This file provides ethical safeguards for expert interviews used to refine the framework. It is not legal advice and does not replace institutional human-subjects review.

## Human-subjects review boundary

If expert interviews are conducted for publishable research, generalizable knowledge, conference presentation, or public release, the project team should consult the relevant institution before collecting data. The team should not assume that expert interviews are automatically exempt.

## Consent / information sheet principles

The information sheet should explain:

- purpose of the project;
- what participation involves;
- expected time commitment;
- voluntary nature of participation;
- how notes and survey responses will be stored;
- whether responses are anonymous, confidential, or attributed;
- how results may be used;
- risks of participation;
- contact information for questions.

## Data minimization

Collect only what is needed:

- pseudonymous ExpertID;
- broad field or role;
- experience band;
- weight survey responses;
- non-identifying feedback themes.

Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## Case-discussion safeguards

Prefer synthetic cases or formally documented public cases. Do not ask experts to decide whether a specific person committed misconduct unless that exact determination is already part of an official public record and the task is limited to interpreting the record.

## Reporting safeguards

Report aggregate findings. Avoid publishing identifiable quotations unless explicit permission has been obtained and local review requirements have been satisfied.

## Institutional Review Before In-Person Interviews

Before conducting in-person interviews, especially if interview data may be used in a manuscript, conference presentation, public repository, or other public research output, the project team should obtain a determination from the relevant institutional review body or human-subjects review office. This protocol does not by itself determine exemption status.
