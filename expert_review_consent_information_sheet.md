# Expert Review Information Sheet Template

**Version:** v1.0.0

## Project purpose

This project develops a transparent research-integrity risk assessment framework. Your feedback will help evaluate module weights, classification boundaries, and safeguards.

## Participation

Participation may include a 30–45 minute interview and a structured weight survey. Participation is voluntary.

## Data collected

The project may collect your broad field, role type, experience band, survey weights, and non-identifying feedback. The project should avoid unnecessary personal information.

## Use of feedback

Feedback may be summarized in aggregate to refine the framework. Identifiable quotations should not be used without explicit permission and appropriate institutional review.

## Important boundary

The interview does not ask you to determine whether any named person committed misconduct.
