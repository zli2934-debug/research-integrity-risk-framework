# Expert Review Methods Plan

**Current package version:** v1.0.0

This manuscript component describes the planned expert-review methodology.

## Planned design

The planned design combines semi-structured interviews with a structured M1–M9 weight survey. Experts will review the purpose of the framework, discuss evidence-module importance, evaluate classification boundaries, and provide proposed weight distributions.

## Planned analysis

Expert weights will be normalized and summarized using mean, median, standard deviation, range, and pairwise disagreement metrics. High-disagreement modules will be flagged for Delphi-style follow-up.

## Reporting boundary

The manuscript should state that expert review is planned or protocol-level unless actual interviews have been completed under appropriate institutional review and citation-verification procedures.
