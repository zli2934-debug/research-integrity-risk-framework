# Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Anomaly | Observed irregularity in paper content or metadata |
| Concern | An anomaly that remains relevant after considering benign explanations |
| Data integrity risk | Risk that reported evidence may not accurately represent underlying data |
| Fabrication | Making up data or results |
| Falsification | Manipulating research materials, equipment, processes, data, or results |
| Image manipulation | Inappropriate alteration or reuse of images that affects interpretation |
| Retraction | Formal removal of trust from the scholarly record for an article |
| Correction | Formal correction of a published article |
| Expression of concern | Journal notice warning readers about unresolved concerns |
| Confirmed misconduct | Officially stated research misconduct-type finding |
| Repeated misconduct pattern | Multiple formal records with confirmed identity, similar patterns, and insufficient benign explanation |
| TRS | Total Risk Score, the weighted module score from 0 to 5 |
