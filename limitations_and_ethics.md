# Limitations and Ethical Safeguards

## Limitations

1. The current weights are pilot expert-prior weights and have not been empirically calibrated.
2. Synthetic benchmark cases test rule implementation but do not prove real-world performance.
3. Keyword-based reason classification may misclassify ambiguous retraction notices.
4. Author-name matching cannot confirm author identity.
5. A database record cannot establish intent, responsibility, or misconduct by itself.
6. Public records may be incomplete, inconsistent, legally constrained, or vague.
7. The framework does not perform image forensics or statistical forensics automatically.
8. The framework is not a substitute for journal, institutional, legal, or professional review.

## Ethical safeguards

- The framework must not be used to publicly accuse individuals of misconduct.
- C5 and C6 classifications require formal record-based evidence.
- C6 requires confirmed identity and clear responsibility, not merely a similar name or coauthorship.
- Reports must distinguish evidence, inference, and formal determination.
- Reports must include limitations and uncertainty.
- Database outputs must include boundary notes.
- All release candidates must undergo second-pass verification.

## Recommended wording

Use:

- "research-integrity risk signal"
- "preliminary risk assessment"
- "database evidence record"
- "requires formal review"
- "official record indicates..."

Avoid:

- "fraudster"
- "proves fraud"
- "guilty"
- "confirmed misconduct" without official wording
- "same author" based only on name matching
